About this prototype

ThreadGrade demonstrates the proposed workflow from textile offcut intake to grading, routing and traceable lot records.

  • All metrics, prices and model scores are illustrative.
  • AI grading, NIR testing, buyer auctions and lender integrations are not connected.
  • Human confirmation is required in the proposed workflow.
  • In a production version, evidence and quality checks would need validation.
